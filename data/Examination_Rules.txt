SMARTCAMPUS UNIVERSITY EXAMINATION RULES
Document Code: SCU-ER-2025-v1

SECTION 1: EXAMINATION ELIGIBILITY

1.1 Examination Eligibility:
A student must satisfy the prescribed attendance and academic requirements to be eligible to appear for the End Semester Examination (ESE).

1.2 Examination Registration:
Students must complete examination registration within the deadline announced by the university.

1.3 Hall Ticket:
Students must carry a valid hall ticket and university identity card to the examination hall.

1.4 Absence from Examination:
Students who fail to attend an examination without an approved reason shall be treated as absent for that examination.

SECTION 2: EXAMINATION CONDUCT

2.1 Examination Hall:
Students must report to the examination hall before the scheduled commencement of the examination.

2.2 Identification:
Students must produce their university identity card and hall ticket when requested by the invigilator.

2.3 Unauthorized Materials:
Students shall not carry unauthorized books, notes, electronic devices, or other prohibited materials into the examination hall.

2.4 Electronic Devices:
Mobile phones, smart watches, programmable calculators, and other unauthorized electronic devices are prohibited unless specifically permitted for the examination.

2.5 Discipline:
Students must follow the instructions given by the invigilator and maintain proper discipline throughout the examination.

SECTION 3: MALPRACTICE

3.1 Examination Malpractice:
Possession or use of unauthorized materials, copying, communication with other candidates, impersonation, or use of unauthorized electronic devices shall be treated as examination malpractice.

3.2 Investigation:
Cases of suspected malpractice may be reported to the appropriate examination authority for investigation.

3.3 Penalty:
Students found guilty of malpractice may receive penalties including cancellation of the concerned examination, cancellation of marks, or other disciplinary action according to university regulations.

3.4 Impersonation:
Impersonation of another candidate in an examination is a serious violation and shall be subject to disciplinary action.

SECTION 4: RE-EVALUATION

4.1 Re-evaluation:
A student may apply for re-evaluation of eligible theory courses after the declaration of examination results.

4.2 Re-evaluation Fee:
The re-evaluation fee is Rs. 750 per subject.

4.3 Application Deadline:
Students must submit the re-evaluation application and prescribed fee within the deadline announced by the university.

4.4 Re-evaluation Result:
The result after re-evaluation shall be processed according to the applicable examination regulations.

SECTION 5: BACKLOG EXAMINATIONS

5.1 Backlog Examination:
Students who fail a course may appear for the corresponding backlog examination during the next available examination cycle.

5.2 Backlog Registration:
Students must register for backlog examinations within the prescribed registration period.

5.3 Backlog Fee:
Applicable examination fees must be paid before the deadline for backlog examination registration.

5.4 Course Clearance:
A backlog course shall be considered cleared only after the student successfully passes the required examination.

SECTION 6: SUPPLEMENTARY AND FAST-TRACK EXAMINATIONS

6.1 Supplementary Examination:
The university may conduct supplementary examinations for eligible students.

6.2 Summer Fast-Track Examination:
Eligible students may be permitted to clear selected backlog courses through summer fast-track examinations.

6.3 Eligibility:
Eligibility for supplementary or fast-track examinations shall be determined according to the applicable university regulations.

SECTION 7: EXAMINATION RESULTS

7.1 Result Declaration:
Examination results shall be published through the official university communication channels.

7.2 Grade Award:
Grades shall be awarded according to the prescribed grading system.

7.3 Result Correction:
Students who identify an apparent error in their examination results may submit a request for verification within the prescribed period.

7.4 Academic Record:
Final examination results shall be recorded in the student's academic record after completion of the applicable verification and evaluation procedures.

SECTION 8: EXAMINATION ATTEMPTS

8.1 Maximum Attempts:
Students shall be permitted to attempt failed courses within the maximum academic duration allowed for completion of the B.Tech program.

8.2 Failure to Clear:
Students who fail to clear a course within the permitted duration shall be governed by the applicable university regulations.

8.3 Improvement Examination:
Eligible students may apply for improvement examinations where such examinations are permitted by the university.
